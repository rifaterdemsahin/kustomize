Kustomize: Bases & Overlays

A 90-second animation walking through why Kubernetes manifests get duplicated across environments, and how Kustomize fixes it with a single base and per-environment overlays.

Watch the animation

Why Kustomize is needed

Without it: copy-paste drift

Three near-identical deployment.yaml files for dev, staging, and prod. Almost every line is shared, but two lines actually differ per environment: replicas and the image tag. A shared change means editing three files — three chances to drift, and three places a typo can hide.

With it: one base, many overlays

Kustomize keeps a single base/deployment.yaml as the source of truth. Each environment gets a tiny overlays/<env>/kustomization.yaml that holds only its diff — a handful of lines instead of a full duplicate manifest. The base never knows its overlays exist.

Strategic merge, not templating

Patches are matched to their target by kind + name, and list items (like containers) are merged by name. There's no templating language to learn — it's plain YAML in, plain YAML out, with Kustomize computing the merge.

No new tooling to run apps

Kustomize is built into kubectl. kubectl apply -k overlays/prod renders the merged manifest and applies it directly — no extra CLI, no rendering step to remember, no secrets stored in a templating engine's values file.

The pattern, in code

base/
  deployment.yaml
  kustomization.yaml

overlays/
  dev/
    kustomization.yaml
  staging/
    kustomization.yaml
  prod/
    kustomization.yaml
    env.yaml
